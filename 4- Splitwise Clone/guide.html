💰 Guide: Smart Expense Splitter

Manage shared budgets and split bills effortlessly.

🔍 What is this tool?

This is a group budget manager designed to keep track of shared expenses. Whether it's a trip, shared apartment bills, or dinner with friends, our tool calculates exactly who owes whom, helping you maintain balanced finances.

🚀 How to Use

  1. Create a group and add all members involved in the expenses.
  2. Log new expenses, specifying the amount and who paid for what.
  3. View the "Balances" tab to see real-time settlements and total debt summaries.

🏆 Gamification Reward

Successfully settle your first group expense to prove your financial responsibility and earn +25 XP points!
