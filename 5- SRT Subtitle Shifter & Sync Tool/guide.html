⏱️ SRT Subtitle Shifter & Sync Tool

This tool allows you to precisely shift your subtitle files (.srt) forward or backward to perfectly match your video's audio.

Instructions:

  • Upload your .srt subtitle file.
  • Enter the shift duration in seconds (e.g., enter 2.5 to shift forward, or -2.5 to shift backward).
  • Click "Sync & Download" to get your perfectly synchronized file.
