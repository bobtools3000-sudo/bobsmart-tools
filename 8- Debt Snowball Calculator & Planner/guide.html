🍅 Pomodoro Focus Timer

A smart time-management timer utilizing the scientific Pomodoro technique to alternate work and breaks.

Instructions:

  • Set your focus timer (typically 25 minutes).
  • Start the timer and stay focused on your task.
  • Take a short break when the alarm goes off.
