📱 QR Code Generator & Scanner Guide

This tool allows you to create custom QR codes for URLs or text and scan existing QR code images to extract their content.

How to use:

  • To Generate: Paste your URL or text into the input field and click "Create QR Code".
  • To Scan: Click "Scan QR Code Image" to upload an existing code from your device.
🏆 Gamification: Successfully create or scan your first QR code to earn +15 points!
